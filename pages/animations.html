Robot dance

For my animation class I rigged a robot from scratch and animated a 30-second dance. I hand-keyed the choreography from a reference video, added electric sparks with a particle system, and rendered it with six cameras.

Final animation · 30 seconds Read the report (PDF) ↗  ·  YouTube ↗
01 · Rigging

Rig from scratch

A Blender armature with consistent left/right naming, IK on arms and legs with pole targets, plus a root bone and a hip control to make posing easier.

02 · Expression

Face controls

Three extra bones drive the eyes and mouth, so gaze and exaggerated expressions animate independently from the body.

03 · Weights

Clean deformation

Weight painting with subtract, blur and smear brushes, with extra care on the joints so nothing breaks while the robot moves.

04 · Animation

Hand-keyed dance

Every pose keyed manually on the armature, following a choreography reference frame by frame in the Dope Sheet and Graph Editor.

05 · Simulation

Electric sparks

A particle system with short-lived, randomized sparks, timed to fire on the sharp, abrupt moves of the dance.

06 · Cameras

Six cameras

A simple office I modeled, shot with six cameras rendered in passes. A small joke: a second robot plays the camera operator.
