Project 01 · Academic exercise

Human anatomy study

A school exercise: recreate a real person's physique as a 3D model in Blender. I chose a bodybuilder (Arnold Schwarzenegger) so I could study every major muscle group, how proportions change between views, and how small details make a body believable.

Turntable · academic exercise YouTube ↗
01 · Base mesh

One vertex, one human

Started from a single vertex with Mirror, Skin and Subdivision modifiers, building a generic body to get the proportions roughly right.

02 · Body

Chest, back and legs

Sculpted the big forms first, then checked them against the reference with overlays and an eight-section proportion grid.

03 · Head

Face from a blank form

The base had no face at all. I prioritized structure first, so the eyelids and neck area are the parts I'd push further.

04 · Hands

Built from a plane

A new workflow for me: I normally start from a cube. Here the palm came from a plane, with fingers and thumb extruded out of it.

05 · Sculpt

Merge and refine

Merged the pieces into one surface and smoothed the transitions, then added nails with Solidify and Subdivision.

06 · Details

Creases and calluses

Small details like calluses below the fingers are what make a model feel believable.

07 · Feet

Same method, new part

Extruded and pulled faces for the general shape and toes, then moved on to the sides and the top.

08 · Detail

Veins and tendons

Sculpted veins and muscle over the foot so it matches the reference's character.

Honest reflection

The model isn't perfect, and I'm happy to say so. The face needs more definition and the muscle groups can be pushed further. Next steps are finishing the face, refining the muscles and adding textures. I feel far more comfortable sculpting than when I started, and that's the point of the exercise.
