Featured Project

Royale Mashine

Found as a single 2D concept on Pinterest, no turnaround sheet, no orthographic breakdown, just one illustration and an artist's note about a spinning crown. Everything past that point was a guess made in three dimensions.

Full pipeline from a lone reference: modeled, textured, rigged and animated by reasoning out the forms the sketch only implied. It's the piece that best represents how I actually work, translating someone else's flat idea into something that stands, turns, and holds a pose.
