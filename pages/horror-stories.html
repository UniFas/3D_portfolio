Why I tell them

I've been a horror fan since I was very young, in movies and in stories, the ones that stay with you long after the lights go out.

Add a love for 3D, animation and storytelling, and the plan is simple: make my own stories, publish them on YouTube for the world to see, and maybe give a few people some shivers.

Story 02 · Animatic

Cam 4

A night-shift security guard, one hour from the end of his shift. The gate bell rings. On the outside camera is a colleague he knows, with a smile that is far too wide. He lets him in. What walks through the gate on Camera 4 is not his colleague. A parasite has taken him as a host and is now taking root in his head, which hangs from a long, stretched neck. This is the animatic: a timing and sound test with placeholder visuals while I sculpt, rig and animate the creature.
